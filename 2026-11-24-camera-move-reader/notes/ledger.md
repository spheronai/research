- 18:05 UTC: bug found in `harness/moves.py`. With `require_heldout=True`, a draw of the empty move or a one-primitive move could never contain a held-out pair, so the sampler raised and Blender quit. test_combos rendered only 6 of 500 clips. Fix: a held-out clip always gets two or more primitives. Draws for every other split are unchanged (the fix only runs when `require_heldout=True`), and the 6 clips already rendered came from draws the fix leaves the same. test_combos re-rendered with `harness/run_combos.sh` in parallel with train. Checked: all 500 test_combos draws now contain a held-out pair.

- 18:57 UTC, before any test scoring: scorer dry run on val (debugging only, not a result). Two scorer rules added. (1) A returned camera that no lens can have (focal <= 0 or non-finite, depth <= 0) counts as a failed clip, and the rate is reported as `unusable_camera_rate`; COLMAP per-image focal produced these on val. (2) A reference row `REF_always_static` (camera never moves, card says static) so naming accuracy can be read against what saying "no move" scores.
